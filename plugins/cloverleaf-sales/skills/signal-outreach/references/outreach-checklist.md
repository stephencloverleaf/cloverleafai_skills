# Outreach checklist

Distilled from the house AI sales communication playbook. Use when no `sales-communication`
or `humanizer` skill is loaded in the session.

## Hard fails: do not send if any of these is true

- A fabricated or weakly inferred fact is presented as known.
- A quote lacks verified wording, speaker, source, date, or context, or a template's example
  name, date, organization, or metric is used as evidence.
- A metric appears with no definition, time period, source, or denominator.
- A customer name, logo, or result appears without permission or an approved public source.
- A platform figure is stale. Never print "45,000+ agencies"; the website says 70,000+.
- The message asks for several unrelated actions, or the copy could be sent unchanged to
  almost anyone in that role.
- The message violates an opt-out request, applicable law, or platform policy.
- The subject or sender identity is deceptive, including a fake `Re:` or `Fwd:`.

## The five questions

Why me. Why now. Why care. Why believe you. What next. Compact structure: context,
consequence, credible proof, one ask.

## Preflight

- [ ] The subject is honest, specific, and scannable on a phone, and the first line carries
      the strongest verified reason.
- [ ] The recipient's priority appears before the product, and personalization is tied to
      what the person is accountable for rather than a fact about them.
- [ ] Every claim, quote, date, number, and link is verified, and an inference is labeled as
      an inference rather than stated as fact.
- [ ] There is value before the ask, and exactly one recipient-facing action that fits a cold
      relationship.
- [ ] The email is 50 to 100 words, reads cleanly on mobile with no images, and makes sense
      if it is forwarded on its own.
- [ ] No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no superlatives, and "Cloverleaf AI" is written
      exactly, never shortened or restyled.
- [ ] The draft sounds like a knowledgeable peer, not a template.

If a required input is missing, mark the draft **NOT READY TO SEND** and name the gap. Ask a
human to review anything touching pricing, ROI, legal language, or a public quote.
